About · KyungJun Lee 이경준

Turning complex technology into experiences anyone can use

Principal UX Designer with 21 years at Samsung Electronics — 9 as a software engineer, 12 in UX — turning complex technology into experiences anyone can use. Now leading GenAI chatbot UX and AI-driven design automation. Built Samsung's first Digital Twin and spatial IoT products from 0 to 1, with 6+ global design awards and 13+ granted patents. Finds business opportunities through research and steps into the UX PM role when needed.

  1. 012005 – 2014

    Depth in technology, as an engineer

    Nine years building mobile protocols, system software and SIM/eSIM. Led Samsung's first eSIM with Sprint and verified in the field across the US, Europe and Asia.

  2. 022014 – 2022

    The human view, in UX

    Moved to UX, covering concept, design and working prototypes on my own. Led the multi-device connection experience (MDE), then found B2B opportunities through Korea–US research and turned them into the smart-apartment business.

  3. 032023 – now

    0→1 and the AI shift, as a Principal

    Built spatial IoT solutions from 0 to 1 — SmartThings Map View, Samsung's first Digital Twin and SmartThings Pro Interactive View. Now leading GenAI chatbot UX and AI-driven design automation.
