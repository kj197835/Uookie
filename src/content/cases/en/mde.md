  problem: Connecting devices gets harder as the number of devices grows — it had to become easy for anyone.
  context:
    - title: Setup was the top complaint
      body: As wearables, audio and IoT products multiplied, users found first connection (OOBE) and setup the most frustrating part.
    - title: Every division did it differently
      body: The Mobile, Home Appliance and Visual Display divisions each connected devices differently, making a consistent experience hard.
    - title: Wireless tech is hard
      body: Wireless technologies like Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and hotspot are unfamiliar to users, starting with their setting names.
