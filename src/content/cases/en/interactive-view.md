background:
  problem: Entering the building-management (BMS) market as a latecomer against established players, we needed a clear differentiator that shows real spaces accurately.
  context:
    - title: A latecomer
      body: Established solutions already own the building-management market, so a feature list alone can't differentiate. We needed a space-first solution that shows the building exactly as it is and lets you manage it right there.
    - title: Operations at scale
      body: Building operators manage many floors, spaces and devices at once.
    - title: Mixed devices
      body: B2B IoT and B2C (SmartThings) devices, robots, ships and vehicles all have to be handled in one place.
approach:
  heading: Redesigning a spatial experience proven in B2C for B2B operations
  items:
    - title: On board from the start
      body: Joined SmartThings Pro from its early days and set the direction of extending the B2C Map View experience to B2B.
    - title: Built for operators
      body: Added editing and filtering to fit the efficiency needs of large-building operators.
    - title: Space as a platform
      body: Designed the 3D map as a shared space that carries not only IoT devices but robots, ships, vehicles and occupancy data.
    - title: Showing value at exhibitions
      body: Designed and ran the CES 2025 booth concept and exhibition screens to show the differentiator live.

    points:
      - title: 2D plan → 3D map, automatically
        body: AI analyzes a building's 2D plan and converts it into a 3D map. Operators see devices on the real layout, not in a list.
      - title: B2B and B2C devices in one space
        body: Places and manages B2B IoT and B2C (SmartThings) devices on the same 3D map, with editing and filtering for large buildings.
      - title: Occupancy heat map
        body: Heat-map sensors (presence) and CCTV (crowd levels) paint an occupancy heat map on the 3D map, applied to energy saving.
      - title: Node-RED-based B2B automation
        body: A Node-RED-style tool that connects devices, conditions and actions with lines, so complex B2B automation can be built visually.
